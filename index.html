How to Pronounce Past Tense "-ed"

The pronunciation of regular past tense verbs depends on the final sound of the base verb. All ending rules below are organized in strict alphabetical order.

Found 0 matching items Click speaker icons to hear pronunciation
/t/

Unvoiced Sound

Your vocal cords do not vibrate at the end of the base verb. The -ED sounds like a sharp "T".

/d/

Voiced Sound

Your vocal cords vibrate at the end of the base verb (includes all vowel sounds). The -ED sounds like a soft "D".

/ɪd/

Extra Syllable

Used only when base verb ends in T or D. Adds an extra syllable ("id" or "ed").
